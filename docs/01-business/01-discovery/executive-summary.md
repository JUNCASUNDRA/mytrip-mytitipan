## 1. Brief Summary

**My Trip My Titipan** is a trust-driven social commerce platform that reimagines the traditional Jasa Titip (jastip) experience in Indonesia. Unlike conventional marketplaces that focus primarily on products and transactions, My Trip My Titipan focuses on people, relationships, and trust as the foundation of commerce.

The platform connects travelers, creators, and shoppers through a community-driven ecosystem where users can discover products, follow trusted jastipers, engage with travel content, and shop through people they trust rather than anonymous sellers.

By combining elements of Social Commerce, Creator Economy, and Travel Commerce, My Trip My Titipan transforms jastip from an informal activity conducted through private chats into a structured, transparent, and scalable digital ecosystem.

---

## 2. Background

The rapid growth of social commerce has fundamentally changed how consumers discover and purchase products. Consumers increasingly rely on recommendations from creators, communities, and trusted individuals rather than traditional advertisements.

At the same time, Jasa Titip (jastip) has become a popular shopping alternative in Indonesia, enabling consumers to access products that are difficult, expensive, or unavailable through conventional retail channels. Many travelers, content creators, and niche product enthusiasts have built informal jastip businesses through social media platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, and Telegram.

Despite its popularity, the jastip ecosystem remains fragmented and largely unmanaged. Transactions depend heavily on personal trust, communication occurs through unstructured chat channels, and there is limited visibility throughout the purchasing process.

As a result, both customers and jastipers face challenges that hinder trust, efficiency, and long-term growth.

My Trip My Titipan was created to address these challenges by providing a dedicated platform designed specifically for social jastip, where trust, transparency, and community become the foundation of every interaction.

## 3. Problems Solved

### Current Ecosystem Challenges

| # | Problem | Impact |
| - | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lack of trust between customers and jastipers | Customers hesitate to transact with new jastipers |
| 2 | High risk of fraud and transaction disputes | Financial losses and negative user experiences |
| 3 | Unstructured order management through chat applications | Miscommunication, incorrect orders, operational inefficiency |
| 4 | Limited visibility throughout the purchasing process | Customer uncertainty and poor experience |
| 5 | Difficulty building reputation for new jastipers | High entry barriers and slow business growth |
| 6 | Lack of specialization discovery | Customers struggle to find trusted experts in specific product categories |

### Root Cause

The core issue within the current jastip ecosystem is the absence of a standardized trust infrastructure.

Without verifiable trust mechanisms, both customers and jastipers are forced to rely on personal assumptions, screenshots, and follower counts as indicators of credibility.

## 4. Solution Offered

My Trip My Titipan provides an integrated platform that combines trust infrastructure, social interaction, and commerce functionality.

### Trust Infrastructure

* Identity Verification (KYC)
* Verified Jastiper Profiles
* Dynamic Reputation System
* Expertise Badges
* Customer Reviews & Ratings

### Commerce Infrastructure

* Structured Product Request System
* Transparent Offer & Pricing Workflow
* Escrow-Based Payment Protection
* Real-Time Order Tracking
* Transaction Management Dashboard

### Social Commerce Features

* Visual Content Feed
* Trip Sharing & Trip Planner
* Live Shopping Sessions
* Follow & Community Features
* Personalized Recommendations

Together, these features create an environment where trust can be established, transactions can be completed securely, and communities can grow organically.
