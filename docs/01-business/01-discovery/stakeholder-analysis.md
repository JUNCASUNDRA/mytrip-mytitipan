# 3. Primary Stakeholders

Primary stakeholders are the main users who directly create and receive value within the platform.

---

## 3.1 Lifestyle Shoppers

### Description

Consumers who want access to products that are difficult, expensive, limited, or unavailable in their local market.

### Examples

- Beauty enthusiasts
- K-Pop fans
- Anime collectors
- Sneaker enthusiasts
- Luxury shoppers
- Gadget enthusiasts

### Goals

- Access unique international products
- Purchase products from trusted sources
- Receive transparent pricing
- Track orders in real time
- Reduce fraud risks

### Pain Points

- Difficulty finding trustworthy jastipers
- Fraud and scam concerns
- Limited visibility into traveler availability
- Unclear pricing and fees
- Slow communication

### Value Received

- Verified travelers and creators
- Escrow-based transaction protection
- Real-time order tracking
- Transparent pricing
- Destination-based product discovery

### Influence Level

High

---

## 3.2 Travelers

### Description

Individuals traveling domestically or internationally who can fulfill product requests during their journeys.

### Examples

- Frequent travelers
- Business travelers
- Students abroad
- Digital nomads
- Tourists

### Goals

- Monetize travel activities
- Reduce unused luggage capacity
- Build reputation
- Receive structured requests

### Pain Points

- Managing requests manually
- Tracking customer orders
- Handling multiple conversations
- Building credibility

### Value Received

- Trip Planner
- Structured order management
- Verified reputation system
- Escrow-protected transactions
- Customer discovery tools

### Influence Level

High

---

## 3.3 Creator Jastipers

### Description

Creators, influencers, and niche experts who monetize their audience through travel-based shopping and product recommendations.

### Examples

- Beauty influencers
- Luxury shoppers
- K-Pop creators
- Anime reviewers
- Sneaker collectors
- Travel content creators

### Goals

- Generate additional revenue
- Strengthen audience engagement
- Build authority within niche communities
- Monetize expertise and influence

### Pain Points

- Monetization depends heavily on sponsorships
- Manual request handling
- Lack of commerce tools
- Limited transaction protection

### Value Received

- Creator commerce tools
- Live shopping sessions
- Community-building features
- Reputation system
- Monetization opportunities

### Influence Level

Very High

# 7. Strategic Implications

The success of My Trip My Titipan depends on balancing the needs of multiple stakeholder groups.

The platform must:

- Enable shoppers to discover trusted products and travelers.
- Enable travelers to monetize trips efficiently.
- Enable creators to monetize influence and expertise.
- Build trust through verification, reputation systems, and escrow protection.
- Foster communities that drive discovery and engagement.
- Create sustainable partnerships with logistics, payment, and retail partners.

By aligning stakeholder incentives, My Trip My Titipan can establish a self-reinforcing ecosystem where commerce, community, and travel experiences grow together.
