# Problem Statement: Crisis of Trust and Inefficiency in the Indonesian Jastip Ecosystem

> **Status**: 📝 Draft
> **Last Updated**: 2026-06-01
> **Author**: MyTrip MyTitipan Team

---

## 1. Problem Definition

### Primary Problem

The Indonesian jastip ecosystem lacks a dedicated trust-driven social commerce environment where shoppers, travelers, and creators can build meaningful relationships, establish credibility, and conduct commerce with confidence.

Today, most jastip activities take place across fragmented platforms such as Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Telegram. While these platforms support communication and content sharing, they are not designed to facilitate trust-based commerce, reputation building, transparent order management, or long-term community engagement.

As a result, users experience uncertainty, fragmented workflows, limited visibility, difficulties verifying credibility, and increased risk throughout the jastip journey.

### Secondary Problems

1. **Lack of Trust Infrastructure**

   * Customers struggle to determine whether a jastiper is trustworthy.
   * Trust is often based on follower counts, screenshots, and personal recommendations rather than verifiable records.

2. **Difficulty Building and Verifying Reputation**

   * New jastipers face significant barriers when trying to establish credibility.
   * Customers have limited ways to evaluate expertise, reliability, and past performance.

3. **Fragmented Social and Commerce Experience**

   * Users must switch between multiple platforms for content discovery, communication, payments, and transaction tracking.
   * The shopping journey becomes disconnected and inefficient.

4. **Limited Transparency Throughout the Shopping Journey**

   * Customers often lack visibility into product availability, purchasing progress, travel schedules, and delivery status.

5. **Operational Inefficiency for Jastipers**

   * Order management relies heavily on manual communication and repetitive administrative work.
   * Scaling operations becomes increasingly difficult as transaction volume grows.

6. **High Risk of Fraud and Transaction Disputes**
   * Customers are required to transfer funds directly to individual accounts.
   * There is limited protection when transactions fail or disputes occur.
   * The absence of escrow-based protection increases financial risk for both parties.

## 2. Problem Context

### Current Situation (AS-IS)

The Indonesian Jasa Titip (jastip) ecosystem has grown rapidly alongside the rise of social media, creator culture, and increasing demand for international products.

Today, most jastip activities occur through platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram, and other social channels. Consumers discover products through content creators, community recommendations, and traveler updates before initiating transactions through private messages.

While this model enables flexible and personalized shopping experiences, it also introduces structural limitations. Social platforms are optimized for content discovery and audience engagement, whereas traditional e-commerce platforms are optimized for inventory management and transaction processing.

The jastip ecosystem exists between these two worlds.

As a result, trust must be established manually, reputation is difficult to verify, transactions are fragmented, and operational processes remain inefficient.

The absence of a dedicated social commerce ecosystem for jastip prevents both shoppers and jastipers from realizing the full value of travel-based commerce.

### Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)

| Level              | Why?                                                  | Answer                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Why 1              | Why do customers hesitate to transact with jastipers? | Because trust is difficult to establish and verify.                                                                           |
| Why 2              | Why is trust difficult to verify?                     | Because reputation is primarily based on social signals such as followers, testimonials, and recommendations.                 |
| Why 3              | Why are social signals insufficient?                  | Because they do not provide standardized records of transaction history, expertise, reliability, or customer satisfaction.    |
| Why 4              | Why is there no standardized trust mechanism?         | Because current jastip activities occur across multiple platforms that focus on either content or transactions, but not both. |
| Why 5 (Root Cause) | Why does the ecosystem rely on fragmented platforms?  | Because there is no dedicated social commerce ecosystem specifically designed for trust-driven jastip activities.             |

### Root Cause

The current jastip ecosystem evolved organically on social media platforms that were never designed to support trust-based commerce.

Existing platforms excel at either:

* Content creation and community engagement
* Transaction processing and product fulfillment

However, no platform effectively combines trust building, creator influence, travel-based discovery, community engagement, and commerce workflows within a single ecosystem.

This creates a structural gap where trust must be established manually, reputation is difficult to verify, and commerce activities remain fragmented across multiple tools and platforms. This fragmentation also prevents the implementation of standardized trust mechanisms such as identity verification, reputation systems, and escrow-based transaction protection.

## 5. Existing Solutions and Gaps

| Existing Solution | What It Does Well | Gap / Limitation |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Instagram | Content discovery and audience engagement | No escrow protection, no reputation system, no order management |
| TikTok | Content distribution and creator reach | Limited trust infrastructure and no dedicated escrow workflow for jastip |
| WhatsApp / Telegram | Direct communication | Unstructured transactions, no escrow protection, poor scalability |
| Traditional Marketplaces | Secure payments and buyer protection | Not designed for request-based shopping, creator-driven commerce, or travel-based purchasing |
| Community Forums | Recommendations and discussion | No integrated trust, escrow, transaction, or fulfillment mechanisms |

### Market Gap

Current solutions address only parts of the jastip journey.

No existing platform combines:

* Trust Infrastructure
* Creator Economy
* Travel Commerce
* Social Commerce
* Community Engagement

within a single ecosystem specifically designed for jastip activities.

---

## 6. Desired Outcome (TO-BE)

A trust-driven social commerce ecosystem where shoppers, travelers, and creators can connect through shared interests, build long-term relationships, discover unique products, and conduct commerce with confidence.

In this ecosystem:

* Trust is verifiable.
* Reputation is transparent.
* Communities drive product discovery.
* Creators can monetize expertise and influence.
* Travelers can transform journeys into economic opportunities.
* Transactions occur naturally as an extension of trusted relationships.
* Buyers and sellers are safeguarded throughout the transaction lifecycle.

Rather than focusing solely on products and transactions, the ecosystem focuses on people, trust, and community-driven commerce.
