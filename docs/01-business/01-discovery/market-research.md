## 2. Market Opportunity

### Social Commerce

Social commerce is transforming how consumers discover and purchase products.

Unlike traditional e-commerce where users search for products directly, social commerce begins with content, relationships, recommendations, and trust.

Typical journey:

Content Discovery → Community Validation → Trust Building → Purchase Decision

This behavior creates opportunities for platforms that combine social interaction with commerce capabilities.

---

### Creator Economy

The creator economy continues to grow as individuals monetize expertise, influence, and audience relationships.

Examples include:

* Beauty creators
* Fashion influencers
* Sneaker collectors
* Luxury shoppers
* K-Pop communities
* Anime enthusiasts
* Travel creators

Consumers increasingly trust individuals more than brands when making purchasing decisions.

This trend creates opportunities for creators to become commerce facilitators rather than simply content producers.

---

### Travel Commerce

Travelers have unique access to products, stores, and experiences that are unavailable to local consumers.

As international travel continues to recover and expand, travel-based commerce becomes increasingly attractive.

Travelers can transform planned trips into economic opportunities by:

* Accepting product requests
* Sharing destination discoveries
* Conducting live shopping sessions
* Building communities around travel experiences

---

### Community Commerce

Modern purchasing decisions are increasingly influenced by niche communities.

Examples include:

* K-Pop fandoms
* Anime communities
* Sneaker collectors
* Beauty enthusiasts
* Luxury shoppers
* Gaming communities

Community members often trust recommendations from fellow members more than traditional advertising.

This creates strong opportunities for community-driven commerce experiences.

---

## 3. Emerging Consumer Behavior Trends

### Creator-Led Purchasing

Consumers increasingly purchase products because of who recommends them rather than which brand sells them.

Trust is shifting from brands toward individuals.

Examples:

* Buying skincare recommended by beauty creators
* Purchasing collectibles recommended by hobby communities
* Ordering products through trusted travelers

---

### Experience-Based Commerce

Consumers no longer purchase products alone.

They also purchase:

* Experiences
* Stories
* Recommendations
* Expertise
* Access

This trend favors platforms that enable meaningful interactions between buyers and sellers.

---

### Destination-Based Shopping

Consumers often associate products with specific shopping destinations.

Examples include:

| Destination | Country       |
| ----------- | ------------- |
| Don Quijote | Japan         |
| Olive Young | South Korea   |
| Costco      | Australia     |
| Sephora     | Singapore     |
| Target      | United States |

Rather than searching for products individually, consumers frequently search based on stores and destinations.

This behavior creates opportunities for destination-centric shopping experiences.

---

### Planned Travel Commerce

Travelers increasingly announce future trips through social media.

Consumers then submit product requests before the trip begins.

Examples:

* "Going to Japan next month."
* "Traveling to Seoul next week."
* "Visiting Singapore this weekend."

This creates demand before purchases occur.

As a result, travel plans become commercial opportunities.

Platforms that provide visibility into future trips can better connect shoppers and travelers.
