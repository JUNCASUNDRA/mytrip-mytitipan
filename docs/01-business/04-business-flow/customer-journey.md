# Customer Journey

---

## 1. Overview

The customer journey within "My Trip My Titipan" represents a fundamental shift from traditional search-and-buy e-commerce to an immersive, travel-based social commerce experience. For the Shopper (Customer), the platform acts as a window to the world, allowing them to access exclusive, localized, or cheaper products from around the globe through a trusted network of Travelers (Jastipers).

Unlike conventional marketplaces where users search for an in-stock SKU, our customers often embark on a journey of inspiration. They discover products through content creators, explore destinations through traveler itineraries, join communities sharing niche interests (e.g., K-Pop merchandise, Japanese skincare), and build relationships with reliable shoppers before ever making a transaction. This journey is highly social, fundamentally tied to real-world travel, and heavily reliant on community trust and escrow security.

---

## 2. Customer Journey Stages

The Shopper journey is a continuous loop of discovery, transaction, and community engagement.

1.  **Discovery:** The shopper is inspired by a destination, a creator's content, a trending product, or an upcoming traveler's itinerary on their Personalized Feed.
2.  **Exploration:** The shopper dives deeper into a traveler's profile, browses community discussions, views "Jastip by Spot" locations, or explores live shopping events from abroad.
3.  **Trust Building:** Before committing, the shopper evaluates the traveler's credibility through Identity Verification, Reputation Scores, Expertise Badges, and past community reviews.
4.  **Request & Negotiation:** The shopper submits a specific Product Request. They communicate with the traveler regarding availability, variations, and final pricing.
5.  **Transaction:** Both parties agree on the terms. The shopper funds the transaction via Escrow Payment, ensuring their money is safe until the product is delivered.
6.  **Fulfillment:** The traveler purchases the item during their trip, handles logistics upon return, and the platform provides status updates and tracking.
7.  **Engagement:** The shopper receives the item, confirms delivery, and releases the escrow funds. They leave a review and might share their purchase within the community.
8.  **Loyalty & Advocacy:** The shopper becomes a repeat buyer, joins specific destination or brand communities, and advocates for trusted travelers, potentially inviting friends to the platform.

## 4. Discovery Paths

Customers can enter the platform ecosystem through multiple, interconnected paths:

### Traveler Discovery
The customer finds a traveler who regularly visits a preferred country (e.g., a flight attendant or frequent business traveler to Japan). They follow the traveler to be notified of upcoming trips, regardless of the specific products offered at the moment.

### Destination Discovery
The customer is interested in a specific country (e.g., South Korea). They browse the "South Korea" destination page to see all upcoming trips, trending local products, and active communities related to that region.

### Store Discovery
The customer wants something from a specific overseas physical store (e.g., a specific outlet mall in Paris, or a unique boutique in Tokyo). They use "Jastip by Spot" to find travelers who have added that specific store to their Trip Planner.

### Product Discovery
The customer knows exactly what they want (e.g., a specific limited-edition sneaker). They search for the product and find either travelers currently taking requests for it, or creators who have reviewed it and linked to travelers heading to that destination.

### Community Discovery
The customer joins a niche group (e.g., "European Luxury Bags" or "Thai Skincare Enthusiasts"). Discovery happens organically through peer recommendations, shared wishlists, and group discussions.

### Creator Discovery
A customer watches a video by a Content Creator reviewing a snack from Taiwan. The video integrates seamlessly with the platform, showing which travelers are currently in Taiwan and can accept requests for that exact snack.

## 5. Trust Journey

Trust is the currency of My Trip My Titipan. Because transactions occur across borders and rely on individuals rather than corporations, the platform must systematically build trust at every step.

*   **Verification:** The journey begins with transparent Identity Verification (KYC) for travelers, ensuring shoppers know they are dealing with real, accountable individuals.
*   **Reviews & Reputation:** Quantitative scores and qualitative reviews from past shoppers act as social proof.
*   **Expertise Badges:** Badges like "Tokyo Expert" or "Skincare Specialist" signal to the shopper that the traveler understands the products they are buying, reducing the risk of purchasing the wrong variant.
*   **Escrow Protection:** This is the ultimate safety net. The shopper's funds are held by the platform and only released to the traveler upon successful delivery, eliminating the fear of fraud.
*   **Community Signals:** Endorsements within community groups ("I've used @jastiper_anna 3 times, highly recommend!") provide organic, powerful trust signals.

Without this structured trust journey, the platform devolves into the risky, fragmented state of traditional social media jastip.

## 6. Commerce Journey

The transaction journey bridges the gap between social interaction and formal commerce:

1.  **Product Request:** The shopper initiates the process by sending a structured request, attaching photos, descriptions, and acceptable price ranges to a traveler.
2.  **Price Confirmation:** The traveler reviews the request, calculates the exchange rate, adds their service fee and estimated local shipping, and sends a formal quote back to the shopper.
3.  **Escrow Payment:** The shopper accepts the quote and transfers the funds into the platform's secure Escrow system. The order status changes to "Funded / Pending Purchase".
4.  **Purchase Execution:** The traveler physically buys the item. They may upload a photo of the receipt or the item in-store. The status updates to "Purchased".
5.  **Shipping:** Upon returning to the origin country, the traveler dispatches the item via domestic logistics. The platform integrates the tracking number.
6.  **Delivery:** The domestic courier delivers the package to the shopper's doorstep.
7.  **Confirmation:** The shopper inspects the item, clicks "Confirm Delivery" in the app, which automatically releases the Escrow funds to the traveler's wallet.

## 9. Moments of Truth

Critical moments that heavily influence customer retention and platform trust.

### First Profile View
*   **Description:** The first time a shopper views a traveler's profile to assess credibility.
*   **Business Impact:** Determines if the shopper will initiate a request or bounce.
*   **Product Strategy:** Ensure profiles instantly display verification status, success rates, and recent positive reviews prominently.

### First Product Request
*   **Description:** The shopper submits their first request for an item.
*   **Business Impact:** Tests the usability of the platform. Friction here stops the commerce loop.
*   **Product Strategy:** Make the request form highly visual, intuitive, and allow easy photo uploads and URL scraping.

### First Escrow Payment
*   **Description:** The shopper parts with their money.
*   **Business Impact:** The ultimate test of platform trust.
*   **Product Strategy:** Use clear micro-copy explaining that the money is held safely by the platform and the traveler only gets paid upon delivery.

### First Delivery
*   **Description:** The shopper receives the physical item.
*   **Business Impact:** The core value proposition is realized.
*   **Product Strategy:** Ensure tracking is seamless. Encourage the traveler to pack items securely to ensure a premium unboxing experience.

### First Review
*   **Description:** The shopper rates the traveler and the platform experience.
*   **Business Impact:** Feeds the ecosystem's reputation engine.
*   **Product Strategy:** Gamify the review process or offer minor platform incentives (points) for leaving detailed reviews with photos.

## 10. Drop-off Analysis

| Drop-off Point | Reason | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Post-Discovery** | Cannot find a traveler going to the desired destination soon. | Allow shoppers to post public "Wishlists" that travelers can browse and claim when planning future trips. |
| **Quote Received** | Total price (Item + Jastip Fee + Shipping) is perceived as too high. | Provide historical price data for similar items so shoppers have realistic expectations before requesting. |
| **Payment Step** | Unfamiliarity with the Escrow system; lack of preferred payment methods. | Offer a wide variety of local payment gateways (Virtual Accounts, E-Wallets) and educational pop-ups about Escrow security. |
| **Post-Delivery** | Failure to confirm receipt, delaying traveler payment and review generation. | Implement auto-complete for orders after X days of courier delivery confirmation. |

## 11. Loyalty Journey

The goal is to transition a one-time shopper into an active ecosystem participant:

*   **Repeat Buyers:** Shoppers who use the platform regularly for their international needs, trusting the Escrow system over traditional DMs.
*   **Community Members:** Shoppers who join niche groups (e.g., "Snack Hunters") to discuss trends, share recommendations, and pool requests.
*   **Advocates:** Shoppers who follow specific travelers closely, turning on notifications for their trips and building a preferred-shopper relationship.
*   **Referrers:** Shoppers who invite friends to the platform using referral codes, driven by the unique ability to get otherwise inaccessible products safely.

## 13. Strategic Insights

*   **Growth via Discovery:** Growth relies on content. The platform must feel alive with destinations, trending products, and live travel updates, not just static listings.
*   **Trust as the Product:** Escrow and Identity Verification are not just features; they are the primary value proposition that pulls users away from unsecure Instagram/WhatsApp transactions.
*   **Community Builds Liquidity:** By grouping shoppers into communities based on interest (e.g., K-Beauty), travelers can easily gauge demand and fulfill bulk orders, increasing marketplace liquidity.
*   **Travel Planning is Commerce Planning:** By knowing where travelers are going *before* they go, the platform can proactively match them with shopper demand, maximizing the traveler's revenue and the shopper's chance of fulfillment.
