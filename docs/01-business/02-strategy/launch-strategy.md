# Go-To-Market & Launch Strategy

> **Status**: 📝 Draft
> **Last Updated**: 2026-06-06

This document outlines the Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy for the My Trip My Titipan MVP launch, focusing on establishing initial marketplace liquidity and mitigating early-stage risks.

## 1. The "Thin Market" Liquidity Problem

A common failure mode for new marketplaces is launching globally on day one, resulting in a "thin market." If a Shopper wants an item from Germany, but we only have Travelers going to Singapore, the Match Rate drops to zero, and the Shopper churns permanently.

**Core Philosophy:** Density > Breadth. We must ensure that wherever demand exists, supply is already waiting.

## 2. Constrained Launch Plan

To guarantee high match rates, the MVP will launch with extreme geographic constraints.

### Phase 1: Single Corridor (Months 1-3)
* **Target Route:** **Japan ↔ Indonesia** (Specifically: Tokyo/Osaka to Jakarta).
* **Why Japan?**
  * Extremely high demand for exclusive products (skincare, anime merchandise, electronics, exclusive sneakers) that are either unavailable or heavily marked up in Indonesia.
  * Predictable supply of Indonesian tourists and diaspora traveling back to Jakarta.
* **Goal:** Master the logistics, quoting workflow, customs nuances, and liquidity of this single corridor before expanding. Achieve a Request Match Rate of > 70%.

### Phase 2: Hub Expansion (Months 4-6)
* **New Routes:** Add **South Korea (Seoul)** and **Singapore**.
* **Goal:** Leverage existing users to cross-pollinate demand for K-Pop/K-Beauty and fast-moving consumer goods from Singapore.

## 3. Supply Acquisition Strategy (The "Chicken" Problem)

In our marketplace, Trusted Travelers are the supply. Without them, there is no product.

* **Target Audience:** Flight attendants, corporate consultants, and frequent vacationers heading to Japan.
* **Acquisition Channels:**
  * Direct outreach in credit card miles/frequent flyer Facebook groups.
  * Partnerships with boutique travel agencies.
* **The "First Trip" Subsidy:**
  * We will subsidize the first 50 Trusted Travelers.
  * Guarantee them a minimum payout or waive their platform fees entirely for their first fulfilled trip to ensure they have a flawless first experience and spread the word.

## 4. Demand Acquisition Strategy

Once a baseline of 50 active, verified trips to Japan is published, we will activate the demand side.

* **Target Audience:** Japan enthusiasts, beauty collectors, and sneakerheads in Jakarta.
* **Acquisition Channels:**
  * **Traveler-Led Growth:** Encourage Travelers to share their "Trip Link" directly to their own Instagram Stories.
  * **Community Partnerships:** Partner with 2-3 mid-tier niche communities (e.g., local Gundam/Anime fan clubs, Japanese skincare forums) by offering exclusive Escrow fee discounts.

## 5. Platform Disintermediation Mitigation (The WhatsApp Leak)

A critical risk is that a Shopper and Traveler connect on the platform, use Escrow once to build trust, and then move to WhatsApp for their next trip to avoid platform fees.

**Strategic Moats:**
* **Convenience > Cost:** The platform must provide value that WhatsApp cannot. We will focus on the Quoting Workflow and Order Dashboard to save Travelers hours of spreadsheet management.
* **Logistics Partnerships:** In the future, offer heavily discounted domestic shipping rates (via logistics partnerships like JNE/Sicepat) that are only available if the transaction happens on-platform.
