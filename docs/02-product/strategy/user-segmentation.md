# User Segmentation

> **Status**: 📝 Draft
> **Last Updated**: 2026-06-06

This document defines the sub-segments of our core user personas. Understanding these segments helps tailor the marketing approach and the level of product hand-holding required.

## 1. Traveler Segments (Supply)

Travelers supply the marketplace with inventory (their luggage capacity). They are segmented by their experience level and frequency of travel.

### A. Casual Traveler
* **Definition:** Travels 1-2 times a year for vacation.
* **Behavior:** Wants to subsidize their holiday. Doesn't have an established follower base.
* **Product Needs:** High hand-holding. Needs guidance on customs limits, pricing suggestions, and step-by-step onboarding.

### B. Frequent Traveler
* **Definition:** Travels 4+ times a year (e.g., consultants, corporate workers, flight attendants).
* **Behavior:** Highly familiar with airports and customs. Uses jastip as a consistent side-hustle.
* **Product Needs:** Efficiency tools. Needs structured request forms and quoting tools to avoid WhatsApp chaos.

#### Subsegment: Route Specialist
* **Definition:** Frequently travels on the same route (e.g. Jakarta ↔ Tokyo every month).
* **Behavior:** Builds expertise and reputation around a destination.
* **Product Needs:** Repeat customer management, trip templates, and route badges.

### C. Creator Traveler
* **Definition:** Travels to create shopping content and curates products for their audience.
* **Behavior:** Has an existing follower base. Focuses on content-driven shopping and curation.
* **Product Needs:** Storefront features, audience interaction tools, and sharing links.

## 2. Shopper Segments (Demand)

Shoppers generate demand. They are segmented by the product verticals they are passionate about. These verticals dictate which communities we should target for acquisition.

### A. Beauty & Skincare
* **Interests:** Japanese drugstores, Korean skincare exclusives, limited-edition cosmetics.
* **Behavior:** High repeat purchase rate. Driven by influencer reviews and trends.

### B. Pop Culture & Fandoms (K-Pop/Anime)
* **Interests:** Concert merchandise, photocard sets, limited edition Gundam models, exclusive cafe collaborations.
* **Behavior:** Highly active in niche communities (Twitter, Discord). Willing to pay high premiums for exclusives.

### C. Fashion & Sneakers
* **Interests:** Exclusive sneaker drops in Japan, local streetwear brands in Korea, luxury goods in Europe.
* **Behavior:** Extremely wary of counterfeits. Values the "Trusted" aspect and Escrow protection the most.

### D. Luxury & Boutique
* **Interests:** High-end bags, watches, boutique jewelry from Europe or specialized hubs.
* **Behavior:** Low volume, extremely high transaction value. Requires enhanced trust and transparency. Full premium protection mechanisms may be introduced in future phases.

### E. General Shopper
* **Interests:**
  - Snacks
  - Souvenirs
  - Daily products
  - Drugstore products
* **Behavior:**
  - Low frequency purchases
  - Price sensitive
  - Usually buys because a friend or traveler is already going abroad
* **Product Needs:**
  - Simplicity
  - Transparent pricing
  - Easy request submission

### F. Family Shopper
* **Interests:**
  - Vitamins
  - Baby products
  - Milk formula
  - Health supplements
  - Daily necessities
* **Behavior:**
  - Trust-oriented
  - Repeat purchases
  - Less trend-driven
* **Product Needs:**
  - Verified travelers
  - Transparent pricing
  - Reliable delivery

## 3. Excluded Segments

### Bulk Resellers
* **Definition:** Businesses using travelers as import channels for large quantities of goods.
* **Reason:** Creates customs risk, increases operational complexity, and deviates from the platform's traveler-to-shopper commerce model.
* **Status:** Out of scope for MVP.

### Commercial Importers
* **Definition:** Businesses seeking to use travelers as recurring import channels.
* **Reason:** Tries to abuse traveler allowances for commercial scale, causing high customs exposure and support overhead.
* **Status:** Out of scope.

## 6. Initial Market Focus

To maximize marketplace liquidity during MVP, My Trip My Titipan will focus on:

1. **Japan**
2. **Korea**
3. **Singapore**

These destinations provide:
- High traveler frequency
- Strong shopper demand
- Established jastip behavior
- Lower operational complexity

Expansion to Thailand, Malaysia, Europe, and the United States will occur after validating the core marketplace loop.

---

## 7. Marketplace Seeding Strategy

The marketplace will prioritize supply acquisition before demand acquisition (solving the cold start problem).

### Phase 1: Supply Seeding
* **Target:** Acquire and onboard Frequent Travelers, Route Specialists, and existing informal jastipers.
* **Goal:** Reach a minimum threshold of 50 active trips/month to ensure initial catalog depth.

### Phase 2: Demand Matching
* **Target:** Expand shopper acquisition by leveraging traveler-shared links, direct social media referrals, and niche community outreach.

### Phase 3: Creator Amplification
* **Target:** Onboard Creator Travelers to broadcast trip storefronts to wider audiences, accelerating network effects and scaling platform transaction volume.
