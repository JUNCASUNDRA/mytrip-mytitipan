# Product Vision

## 1. Vision Statement

To become the most trusted travel-based commerce platform in Southeast Asia, connecting travelers and shoppers through secure, request-driven cross-border shopping.

My Trip My Titipan transforms the informal *Jasa Titip* (jastip) practice into a structured, secure, request-based shopping experience. We empower travelers to monetize their travel plans and shoppers to access global products through individuals they trust, all seamlessly enabled by a robust, invisible trust infrastructure.

## 2. Product Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Discovery Through Travel** | The Trip Planner is the beating heart of our ecosystem. Shopping is driven by destinations, trips, itineraries, and curated *Jastip by Spot* locations. We organize commerce around where people are traveling and what unique experiences they can offer. |
| **Request-Driven Commerce** | We champion intentional, curated shopping over infinite scrolling. The structured Product Request is our core capability, allowing shoppers to dictate demand and enabling dynamic matching with travelers heading to the right destinations. |
| **People Over Inventory** | We connect individuals to individuals, not users to warehouses. The platform prioritizes traveler expertise, creator curation, and community interaction above generic product catalogs or SKU-led marketplaces. |
| **Trust as Invisible Infrastructure** | Escrow payments, traveler reputation profiles, and community reviews act as the foundational trust layer. They protect transactions while keeping onboarding frictionless during early phases. |

## 3. Target Users

| Persona | Role | Needs |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Lifestyle Shoppers** | Primary (Demand) | Seamless access to international, drugstore, family, or limited-edition products. A structured Product Request system to easily articulate their needs, paired with the peace of mind provided by a secure transaction environment. |
| **Trusted Travelers** | Primary (Supply) | A simple Trip Planner to generate shareable links for upcoming journeys, manage incoming Product Requests, and safely monetize excess luggage capacity without the friction of manual chat-based coordination. |
| **Creator Travelers** | Secondary (Growth Supply) | A dedicated platform to leverage audience trust and monetize product expertise. They curate items and share their traveler page to drive community shopping. |

## 4. Problem We Solve

The current jastip ecosystem operates in a highly fragmented state across social media and chat apps, causing significant friction in both discoverability and operational execution.

| Problem | Our Strategic Solution |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Disconnected Travel & Commerce** | We unify travel plans and shopping demand. Our Trip Planner enables travelers to publish trips and share them directly through social channels, creating a structured bridge between travel plans and shopping demand. |
| **Friction in Request-Based Transactions** | We replace chaotic chat negotiations and manual spreadsheet tracking with a structured Product Request engine, standardized quoting workflows, and an intuitive order management dashboard. |
| **Trust Deficit in Peer-to-Peer Commerce** | We mitigate the high risk of fraud and blind bank transfers by embedding Escrow Payments, traveler profiles, transaction history, and community reviews as the core trust loop beneath every transaction. |
| **Absence of Social Proof** | We replace easily manipulated follower counts with a dynamic Reputation System and Expertise Badges, driven entirely by actual completed transactions and verified community reviews. |

## 6. Success Metrics

To ensure we are building a vibrant, travel-driven ecosystem rather than just a payment utility, we will track:

* **Active Trip Supply:** The total number of active trips published by Travelers each month, serving as the foundational supply for the ecosystem.
* **Trip-to-Request Liquidity:** The average number of Product Requests generated per published Trip Planner itinerary.
* **Request Match Rate:** The percentage of Shopper Product Requests successfully matched with and accepted by a Traveler.
* **Ecosystem Retention:** Repeat purchase frequency for shoppers and 3-month active retention for travelers publishing trips.
* **Community Engagement:** The percentage of completed transactions that generate a review, rating, or shared community post.
* **Trust Infrastructure Adoption:** We track Escrow payment conversion rates and review completion rates to ensure the trust loop completes smoothly without friction.

## 7. Product Scope

### In Scope (MVP)

* **Trip Planner:** The core engine allowing Travelers to create and manage itineraries, travel dates, and available capacity.
* **Trip Link Sharing (MVP Discovery):** The primary channel for matching. Travelers share direct links to their specific trips via social media/messaging apps, leading shoppers directly to their request page.
* **Product Request Engine:** Structured forms allowing Shoppers to request specific items, creating clear demand signals.
* **Traveler Profiles & Reputation:** Profiles showcasing traveler identity, upcoming trips, transaction history (completed trips/orders count), and reviews.
* **Quoting & Negotiation Workflow:** Standardized tools for Travelers to respond to Product Requests with transparent pricing.
* **Trust Infrastructure Integration:** Escrow Payment handling (holding funds until confirmed delivery), traveler profile history, and reviews. Identity verification (KYC) is deferred to a future phase.
* **Order Management Dashboard:** Essential status tracking for both parties (Requested, Accepted, Paid, Purchased, Shipped, Delivered).

### Out of Scope

* Centralized internal marketplace discovery feed or traveler listing directory (deferred to V2).
* Native Live Shopping video broadcasting (leveraging existing social channels for MVP).
* Complex AI-driven hyper-personalized recommendation algorithms.
* Direct API integration with international cross-border customs systems.
* Complex community forum structures (e.g., deeply nested threaded discussions).

### Non-Goals

* **We are NOT a fintech or payment gateway:** Escrow is a necessary supporting infrastructure, not our primary value proposition.
* **We are NOT a traditional e-commerce marketplace:** We will not manage warehouses, host static inventory catalogs, or compete on instant local delivery.
* **We are NOT a pure social network:** While we leverage social mechanics, every feature is strategically designed to drive towards a trusted, travel-based commerce outcome.
